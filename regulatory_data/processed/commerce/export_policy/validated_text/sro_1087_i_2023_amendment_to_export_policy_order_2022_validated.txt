GOVERNMENT OF PAKISTAN
MINISTRY OF COMMERCE
*****

Islamabad, the 18th August, 2023.

ORDER

S.R.O 1087(I)/2023. In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, 1950 (XXXIX of 1950), the Federal Government is pleased to direct that the following further amendments shall be made in the Export Policy Order, 2022 namely:-

In the aforesaid Order,-

i) In paragraph 7, sub-paragraphs (6) and (7) shall be omitted; and

ii) In Schedule-II, in column (1), against Serial No. 8 in column (4), the expression “(excluding that manufactured in manufacturing bonds and export oriented unites in case of export to Afghanistan only)” shall be omitted.

[F. No. 4 (3)/2021- Export-III]

(SAMIULLAH KHAN)
Section Officer (Export-II)
