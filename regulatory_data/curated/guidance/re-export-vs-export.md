question: What is the difference between an export and a re-export?
topics: [export, import, re_export]
trade_direction: both
authority_basis: ["Customs Act, 1969", "Export Policy Order, 2022"]

An export is goods leaving Pakistan that were made or produced there. A
re-export is goods leaving Pakistan again after having been imported - the
goods were never really destined to stay, they came in and are now going
back out, sometimes to the same country and sometimes onward elsewhere.

The distinction matters for duty. Goods that were imported under a scheme
like temporary import or bonded warehousing, and are now being re-exported
rather than sold locally, often do not attract the full import duty they
otherwise would have, precisely because they never entered the local market.
Re-exporting is also how you'd typically recover goods that were imported by
mistake, rejected by a buyer, or brought in temporarily for a purpose like
an exhibition.

Because re-export can affect whether duty is owed or refundable, it usually
needs to be declared and processed as such rather than filed as an ordinary
fresh export - the customs history of how the goods entered Pakistan in the
first place matters to how the re-export is treated.
