Pakistan has separate Baggage Rules that let travellers bring in personal
belongings and a limited amount of other goods without going through the
full commercial import process, on the idea that a returning traveller is
not "importing" in the way a business shipment is.

What generally distinguishes personal baggage from a commercial import is
quantity, value, and intent: items that are clearly for personal use, in
reasonable quantities for a traveller rather than for resale, are usually
covered, while anything that looks like it is meant to be sold - large
quantities of the same item, for example - can be treated as a commercial
import regardless of how it is carried.

The exact allowances - what is duty-free, what has a set duty regardless of
value, and the specific limits for things like electronics or gifts - are
set out in the Baggage Rules themselves and change from time to time, so
this general explanation should not be relied on for the current specific
limits. Check with Pakistan Customs directly, or the current Baggage Rules
notification, before travelling with anything beyond ordinary personal
effects.
