The Pakistan Single Window (PSW) is the government's electronic platform for
international trade. Instead of separately contacting customs and every
other agency that might need to approve your shipment - like plant
quarantine, drug regulators, or standards bodies - PSW lets you submit one
electronic declaration that gets routed to all the relevant agencies at
once.

For exporters and importers this means one login, one set of uploaded
documents, and one place to track where your shipment's clearance actually
stands, rather than chasing several separate offices. PSW works alongside
WeBOC, the customs computer system that handles the actual assessment and
clearance of the declaration once it is filed.

PSW is the platform you would use to file things like a Goods Declaration,
apply for Form-E, or request an electronic Certificate of Origin.
