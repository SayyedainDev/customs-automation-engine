Anti-dumping duty is an extra charge added on top of normal customs duty for
specific products from specific countries, meant to counter "dumping" -
where a foreign producer sells goods into Pakistan at a price lower than
what they charge in their own home market, in a way that harms local
industry making the same product.

It is not a general tax that applies to a whole product category from
everywhere; it is investigated and imposed case by case, usually after a
local industry complains to Pakistan's National Tariff Commission, which
investigates whether dumping is actually happening and causing harm before
any duty is imposed. When it is imposed, it applies to specific HS codes
from specific named countries or even specific named exporters, not to the
product generally.

If you are importing a product that has previously been subject to trade
disputes - steel and certain chemicals are common examples worldwide - it is
worth checking the National Tariff Commission's current notifications for
that product and origin country before assuming only the standard tariff
rate applies.
