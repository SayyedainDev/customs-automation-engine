A Goods Declaration, usually just called a GD, is the formal statement you
(or your customs agent) file with Pakistan Customs describing exactly what is
being imported or exported: the goods, their value, their HS code, and the
other shipment details customs needs to assess duty and decide whether to
examine the cargo.

It is filed electronically through the Pakistan Single Window, which then
routes it to WeBOC, the customs computerised system that actually processes
clearance. Once filed, the system may select the shipment for document
checks only, a physical examination, or straight-through clearance,
depending on risk factors the system evaluates.

Getting the GD right matters: the HS code and declared value you enter
directly decide how much duty is calculated, and a GD that does not match
your supporting documents (invoice, packing list) is one of the most common
reasons a shipment gets held for review.
