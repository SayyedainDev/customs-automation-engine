A bonded warehouse is a storage facility licensed by customs where imported
goods can sit without duty being paid yet. The "bond" refers to a financial
guarantee the warehouse operator gives customs, promising the duty will
eventually be paid (or the goods re-exported) rather than disappearing into
the local market untaxed.

This is useful when you are not ready to sell or use imported goods
immediately: instead of paying duty upfront and tying up money in goods
sitting in your own storage, you can keep them bonded and only pay duty when
you actually take them out for local use. If the goods are re-exported
instead of entering the local market, duty may not become payable at all.

Bonded warehousing is also commonly used by export-oriented manufacturers who
import raw materials duty-free under a bond, use them to make finished goods,
and export the finished product - never actually owing the import duty
because the material never entered the local market as such.
