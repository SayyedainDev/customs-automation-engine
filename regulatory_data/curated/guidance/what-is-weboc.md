WeBOC (Web-Based One Customs) is Pakistan Customs' computerised clearance
system - the system that actually assesses a Goods Declaration, calculates
duty and taxes, decides whether a shipment needs examination, and issues
clearance once everything checks out.

Where the Pakistan Single Window is the front door exporters and importers
use to submit a declaration and reach every relevant government agency,
WeBOC is the customs engine working behind that front door specifically for
the customs side of clearance. In everyday conversation people sometimes use
"WeBOC" to mean the whole filing process, but strictly it is the customs
system PSW connects to.

If a shipment is stuck "in WeBOC", it usually means it is somewhere in the
customs assessment, examination, or clearance stage of processing.
