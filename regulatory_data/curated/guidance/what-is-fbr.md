The FBR, the Federal Board of Revenue, is Pakistan's national tax and
customs authority. Pakistan Customs operates as part of the FBR, alongside
the FBR's other main jobs of collecting income tax and sales tax.

This is why customs, sales tax, and income tax questions often trace back to
the same authority: the FBR sets and enforces the Customs Act, the Customs
Rules, and the tariff schedule that decides classification and duty, while
also administering the sales tax and income tax that can apply at the import
stage.

If you need an NTN (National Tax Number) to register as a trader, or you are
dealing with a customs valuation ruling, both come from the FBR.
