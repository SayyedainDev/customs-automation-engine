question: What is the difference between prohibited and restricted goods?
topics: [prohibited_goods, import, export]

Prohibited goods cannot be imported or exported at all - there is no permit
or condition that makes them allowed. Restricted goods can be imported or
exported, but only if you meet a specific condition first, most often
getting a permit or no-objection certificate from the relevant government
agency before customs will release them.

Which category a product falls into is set out in the Import Policy Order
for imports and the Export Policy Order for exports, and it is worth
checking before you commit to a shipment rather than after - a restricted
item without its permit will simply be held at customs, and a prohibited
item cannot be brought in no matter what paperwork you have.

These lists are not fixed forever: products move between freely allowed,
restricted, and prohibited status as policy changes, so checking the current
Import or Export Policy Order for your specific product is more reliable
than assuming its status has not changed since you last checked.
