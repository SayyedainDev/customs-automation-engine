A customs agent, often called a clearing agent in Pakistan, is a licensed
professional who files your customs declarations and manages clearance on
your behalf, so you do not have to deal directly with WeBOC and the customs
process yourself.

In practice this usually covers: classifying your goods under the right HS
code, preparing and filing the Goods Declaration, coordinating with the
shipping line or airline, dealing with customs if a shipment is selected for
examination or review, and generally knowing the current procedural details
that change more often than the law itself does.

Using one is not always legally required for every shipment, but most
businesses - especially first-time exporters and importers - use a customs
agent because the process has enough procedural detail and local knowledge
involved that getting it wrong can mean delays or extra costs. Customs agents
are licensed and regulated, so it is worth confirming an agent's licence is
current before engaging them.
