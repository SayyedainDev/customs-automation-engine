An NTN, National Tax Number, is the identifier the FBR assigns you when you
register as a taxpayer in Pakistan. It is the foundation that most other
trade registrations build on: you generally need one before you can register
as an exporter or importer, open a business bank account for trade payments,
or get set up on the Pakistan Single Window.

Individuals, partnerships, and companies each register slightly differently,
but the NTN itself is the common starting point in all cases. Once you have
one, it is also usually what banks and customs use to identify you across
different filings, rather than treating each shipment as unconnected.

If you are just starting to explore exporting or importing, getting your NTN
in place - and, alongside it, membership of a relevant chamber of commerce
or trade body where required - is typically the first practical step, before
worrying about a specific shipment's paperwork.
