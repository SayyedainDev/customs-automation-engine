TDAP stands for the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan. It operates
under the Ministry of Commerce and exists to help Pakistani businesses
export - promoting Pakistani products abroad, organising trade fairs and
delegations, and providing guidance to new exporters on how the export
process works.

For an individual exporter, TDAP is also often the body that issues or
authorises Certificates of Origin, and it publishes guidance material aimed
specifically at first-time exporters trying to understand documentation and
procedure.

TDAP is a promotion and facilitation body, not the customs authority itself
- customs clearance and duty decisions are the Federal Board of Revenue's
job, while TDAP's role is closer to helping exporters find markets and
navigate the paperwork.
