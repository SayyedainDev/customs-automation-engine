A chamber of commerce is a membership body for businesses in a particular
city, region, or industry sector. For trade specifically, chambers often
play a documentary role: they can attest or issue certain trade documents,
most commonly Certificates of Origin, on behalf of their members.

Beyond documents, chambers of commerce also generally offer things like
business networking, market information, and sometimes act as a
recognised voice representing local businesses' interests to government -
useful context if you are new to an industry and trying to understand who
else exports the same kind of product you do.

Membership is often a practical prerequisite for getting documents attested
through a chamber, so if you plan to rely on a chamber for Certificates of
Origin or similar paperwork, it is worth joining the relevant chamber for
your city or sector before you need the document urgently.
