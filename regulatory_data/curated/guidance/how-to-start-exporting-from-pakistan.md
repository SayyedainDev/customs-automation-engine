Before you can file your first export shipment, there is usually a
registration stage to get through, separate from the paperwork for any one
shipment. In general terms, new exporters need to be a registered taxpayer
with an NTN, register as an exporter and get set up on the Pakistan Single
Window, and often join a relevant chamber of commerce or trade association
for their sector.

Once that foundation is in place, exporting a specific shipment generally
means: agreeing terms and price with your buyer (including which Incoterm
applies), preparing your commercial invoice and packing list, arranging your
Form-E with your bank, filing your Goods Declaration and any Certificate of
Origin through the Pakistan Single Window, and coordinating with your
shipping line or freight forwarder to move the goods.

The exact steps and current portal screens change over time, so for the
precise, up-to-date procedure it is worth going through TDAP's official
guidance for new exporters or the Pakistan Single Window's own user manual,
rather than relying on a general summary like this one - and a licensed
customs agent (clearing agent) can also walk you through your first
shipment.
