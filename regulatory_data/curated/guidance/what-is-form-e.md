Form-E is the form you fill in with your bank whenever you export goods from
Pakistan. Its main job is to record how much you are being paid for the
shipment, so the State Bank of Pakistan can track that export earnings
actually come back into the country.

You normally arrange Form-E through the same bank that is handling your
payment (often tied to your letter of credit), and it is filed electronically
alongside your export declaration on the Pakistan Single Window. Customs will
not let your shipment go through without it, because it is treated as proof
that the export is properly declared and the payment is being tracked.

Think of it less as a customs form and more as a banking record that customs
also checks - it connects your shipment to the money you are supposed to
receive for it.
