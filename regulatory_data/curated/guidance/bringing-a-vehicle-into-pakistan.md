Bringing a vehicle into Pakistan is possible, but it is treated as a distinct
category of import with its own rules, separate from ordinary personal
baggage - a car is not something you casually declare like a suitcase.

There are generally a small number of recognised routes for individuals to
bring in a vehicle: as part of a formal "transfer of residence" scheme for
people relocating back to Pakistan after living abroad, under a gift scheme
where an overseas Pakistani sends a vehicle as a gift, or as a straightforward
commercial import subject to the normal import process. Each route has its
own eligibility conditions, required documents, and duty treatment, and
which one applies depends on your specific situation - how long you lived
abroad, your immigration/residence status, and the vehicle's age and value
all typically matter.

Because vehicle import duty and eligibility rules change relatively often
and the amounts involved are significant, this is one of the areas where it
is especially important to confirm the current rules directly with Pakistan
Customs or a customs agent before committing to shipping a vehicle, rather
than relying on general knowledge of how the schemes used to work.
