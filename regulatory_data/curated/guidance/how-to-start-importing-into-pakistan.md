As with exporting, there is a registration stage before your first shipment:
generally you need an NTN, registration as an importer, and to be set up on
the Pakistan Single Window so you can file declarations electronically.

Before you commit to an order, it is worth checking whether the product you
want to import is freely importable, restricted, or prohibited under the
current Import Policy Order - some categories need a specific licence or
permit from another agency before customs will release them at all.

For a specific shipment, the general flow is: agree terms with your
supplier, arrange payment (often a letter of credit for larger or new trade
relationships), receive your shipping documents (commercial invoice, packing
list, bill of lading or airway bill), file your Goods Declaration through the
Pakistan Single Window with the correct HS code, and pay the duty and taxes
assessed before the shipment is released.

Because import restrictions and required permits vary a lot by product, it
is worth confirming your specific product's status with a customs agent or
the Import Policy Order directly before you commit to an order, rather than
assuming any product can be imported freely.
