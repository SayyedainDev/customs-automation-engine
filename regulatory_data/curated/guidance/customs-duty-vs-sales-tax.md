Several different charges can apply when goods are imported into Pakistan,
and it helps to know they are not the same thing:

Customs duty is the base charge for bringing goods into the country at all,
set by the tariff for that HS code. Regulatory duty is a separate, additional
charge the government sometimes adds on top of customs duty for specific
products - often to protect a local industry or discourage a particular
import, and it does not apply to everything.

Sales tax at import is a version of the same sales tax charged on goods sold
domestically, just collected at the border instead of at the point of sale.
Income tax can also be withheld at import as an advance against the
importer's eventual income tax liability.

Because several of these can stack on the same shipment, the total cost of
clearing goods is usually more than the customs duty rate alone would
suggest - always confirm the exact current rates for a specific HS code
directly with the tariff or a customs agent rather than assuming only one
charge applies.
