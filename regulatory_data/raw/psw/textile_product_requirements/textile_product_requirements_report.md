Scope: Pakistan exports; five approved textile PCT codes

## General customs documents

The official TIPP Customs Clearance Procedure for exports lists:

- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Form-E
- Bill of Entry, only when imported material was used in production
- Export Facilitation Scheme 2021 documentation, only when that scheme is used
- Brand certificate, only when a brand name is declared

The TIPP procedure does not state a general customs-clearance fee or processing time.

## Raw cotton conditions

PCT 5201.0090 is not a simple unrestricted test case. The current official-source chain requires:

- A security deposit equal to 1% of contract value with the State Bank of Pakistan.
- SBP confirmation presented to Customs with shipping documents.
- An irrevocable letter of credit opened by the buyer.
- Completion of the contracted shipment within 180 days thereof.
- Proportionate forfeiture by SBP for the quantity not shipped when the contract is not performed within the stipulated time.
- A DPP phytosanitary certificate.
- An importing-country NPPO import permit when required by the destination country.

TIPP states that the phytosanitary certificate costs PKR 2,500 and normally takes 1–3 working days. Inspection, fumigation or laboratory testing may add requirements or charges; TIPP does not state those additional amounts.

## Conditional certificate of origin

TIPP lists cotton yarn, denim, cotton T-shirts and mill-made cotton bed sheets under its consolidated certificate-of-origin measure. This requirement depends on the destination or preferential trade scheme:

- China under CPFTA: PKR 400 per form; 1 hour to 1 day.
- Other TDAP certificate-of-origin destinations: PKR 400 per printed form, with no issuance fee stated; 1 hour to 1 day.
- GSP/REX registration: no fee stated; 1–2 hours; the TIPP page says “supporting documents” without identifying them.

The selected raw-cotton commodity `520100901000` was not listed in that consolidated TIPP certificate-of-origin commodity set.

## Verification limitations

- The official TIPP keyword measure search returned a database error during retrieval. Exact commodity pages, exact measure commodity-list pages and official procedure pages were checked directly instead.
- `null` in the JSON means that the value was not stated or a requirement was not identified in the reviewed official sources. It must not be interpreted as proof that no destination-country or future requirement can apply.
- TIPP uses 12-digit commodity keys. For raw cotton, PCT 5201.0090 was checked as `520100901000`, specifically “Raw cotton, not carded or combed: Other,” not the separate ginned-cotton variant.
- The JSON is source data for later review. It has not been embedded, inserted into a database or executed as a compliance rule.
